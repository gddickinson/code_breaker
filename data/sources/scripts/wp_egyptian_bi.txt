{{Short description|Hieroglyphs representing two consonants}}
The biliteral [[Egyptian hieroglyphs]] are hieroglyphs which represent a specific sequence of two consonants. The listed hieroglyphs focus on the consonant combinations rather than their meaning.<ref>James P. Allen, ''Middle Egyptian: An Introduction to the Language and Culture of Hieroglyphs'', Cambridge University Press, 15 Apr 2010  [https://books.google.com/books?id=lF78Max-h8MC&pg=PA25 25ff].</ref>
